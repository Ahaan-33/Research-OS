# Research Operating System
# Knowledge Acquisition

Knowledge Acquisition records scientific work in the Research State.

Its objective is faithful capture rather than interpretation.

---

## Capture Once

Scientific information should be captured once.

Every subsequent representation derives from the captured object.

---

## Capture Reasoning

Capture should preserve:

- observations
- assumptions
- decisions
- uncertainty
- context

Scientific reasoning is as valuable as scientific results.

---

## Atomic Capture

Each captured object should represent a single scientific idea.

Atomic objects maximise reuse and synthesis.

See:
- [[Research Information Model]]

---

## Minimise Friction

Capture should occur with minimal interruption to scientific work.

Recording knowledge should never become the primary task.

---

## Preserve Uncertainty

Incomplete understanding should be recorded explicitly.

Unknowns, questions and competing explanations are valid scientific objects.

Uncertainty is resolved through synthesis rather than hidden during capture.

---

## Capture Precedes Organisation

Organisation should emerge from captured knowledge.

Researchers should not decide where information belongs before recording it.

See:
- [[Organisational Spaces]]

---

## Capture is Append-Only

Capture records new knowledge.

It does not modify previous understanding.

Changes to scientific understanding occur during synthesis.

See:
- [[Scientific Synthesis]]
