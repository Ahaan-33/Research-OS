# Research Operating System
# Knowledge Evolution

The Research State evolves through successive cycles of acquisition and synthesis.

Scientific understanding changes without losing provenance.

---

## Current Understanding

The Research State always represents the best current scientific understanding.

It is not an archive.

---

## Provenance

Previous states remain available to explain how current understanding was reached.

History supports interpretation.

It does not replace the current state.

---

## Supersession

Scientific objects are superseded rather than rewritten.

Earlier conclusions remain part of the historical record.

Current conclusions represent present understanding.

---

## Evolution

Scientific progress is cumulative.

Knowledge evolves by refinement rather than replacement.

---

## Reproducibility

Every conclusion should be traceable to the evidence and reasoning that produced it.

Scientific evolution should therefore remain transparent.

---

## Temporal Navigation

Researchers should be able to inspect previous Research States without affecting the current one.

History is a view of the Research State through time.

---

## Living Knowledge

The Research State is continuously updated as new evidence is synthesised.

The present state is authoritative.

Previous states provide context.
