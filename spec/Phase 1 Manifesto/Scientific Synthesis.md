# Research Operating System
# Scientific Synthesis

Scientific Synthesis transforms captured information into scientific understanding.

Unlike Knowledge Acquisition, which records observations, synthesis modifies the Research State.

---

## Purpose

Synthesis integrates:

- observations
- experiments
- literature
- hypotheses
- analyses

into coherent scientific understanding.

---

## Conflict

Contradictory ideas should coexist.

Conflict represents incomplete understanding rather than failure.

Contradictions identify opportunities for investigation.

---

## Integration

Related objects are connected through explicit semantic relationships.

Understanding increases by integrating evidence rather than accumulating information.

See:
- [[Relationship Ontology]]

---

## Resolution

Synthesis may:

- strengthen
- weaken
- refine
- merge
- supersede

existing scientific understanding.

The objective is a more accurate Research State.

---

## History

Previous interpretations remain available as provenance.

Scientific progress should be explainable.

See:
- [[Knowledge Evolution]]

---

## Continuous Process

Synthesis is continuous.

Every new observation has the potential to modify scientific understanding.

The Research State is therefore never complete.

---
## Contradiction as Signal

Contradictory conclusions should not overwrite one another.

Instead, they form explicit regions of conflict within the Research State.

These regions identify unanswered scientific questions and become candidates for future investigation.

Resolution occurs through additional evidence rather than manual deletion.
